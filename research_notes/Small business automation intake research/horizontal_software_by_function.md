# Horizontal (Cross-Industry) Small Business Software by Function: Legacy to Modern (as of Oct 2026)

Method note: 16 tool calls (web searches; one fetch failed because method.me is not in the session's allowed network domains). Most market-share data for SMB software comes from secondary aggregators with unclear methods, and those figures often conflict. Treat percentages as directional. The per-function catalog of tools, and their legacy/mainstream/modern-cloud/AI-native labels, is the researcher's classification from general product knowledge. It is marked as inference and should be spot-checked against vendor sites and the Zapier, Make and n8n directories before it is used in an intake form.

Label key used below: L = legacy (desktop/on-prem/manual), M = mainstream (dominant SMB SaaS/incumbent), C = modern-cloud (newer cloud/API-first), AI = AI-native. Price tier: $ = free to ~$30/mo, $$ = ~$30 to $150/mo, $$$ = $150+/mo or per-employee/enterprise. Integration: Z = native Zapier app, Mk = Make, n8n = native n8n node, API = public API, Closed = partner-gated or no practical API.

## Q1. Which tools dominate SMB usage per function (survey/market-share data)?

### Takeaway
QuickBooks dominates U.S. SMB accounting, with Xero a distant second in the U.S. but a leader in the UK, Australia and New Zealand. Square, Clover and Toast lead POS. ADP, Paychex and Gusto lead payroll. HubSpot is the default SMB CRM, and Salesforce leads the total CRM market. Mailchimp leads email, with Klaviyo the fast-growing challenger in e-commerce. Google Workspace and Microsoft 365 split productivity about evenly. Wix leads hosted site builders, and WordPress leads overall. Zapier has the largest automation catalog. Hard, methodologically sound SMB-specific share data is scarce in every category.

## Q2. What legacy/manual practices are still common (paper, Excel, fax, desktop software, on-prem servers)?

### Takeaway
Paper checks, spreadsheets, paper receipts and filing, and desktop accounting all remain widespread among very small firms. Several legacy products cross support cliffs in 2025–2026, notably QuickBooks Desktop 2023, Office 2016/2019, Exchange 2016/2019, Skype for Business and Publisher. Those cliffs create forced-migration moments. Recent hard data on fax use is absent.

### Cited Findings
- Federal Reserve data (2024): nearly 80% of "very small" firms (revenue under $1M) and 83% of "small" firms use paper checks for business payments. — [Atlanta Fed](https://www.atlantafed.org/blogs/take-on-payments/2026/01/12/why-do-businesses-still-use-paper-checks)
- Another payments-firm study found about 75% check use, per Gene Marks. — [21 Hats / Gene Marks](https://21hats.substack.com/p/are-you-still-writing-paper-checks)
- 58% of organizations reported check fraud in 2025 (AFP), which is a push factor away from checks. — [Beancount summary](https://beancount.io/blog/2026/07/21/check-fraud-2026-afp-survey-small-business-payment-guide)
- Search queries for "where to cash a payroll check" run about 35,000 per year, which suggests paper paychecks persist. — [OnPay](https://onpay.com/insights/payroll-questions-small-businesses-google/)
- Older data: 48% of small businesses with under 100 employees used paper-based systems to organize key data (FileMaker survey). 30% of owners tracked expenses with paper receipts (Manta). — [Small Business Trends](https://smallbiztrends.com/2017/04/manual-process.html)
- 64% of U.S. companies, and 74% of those under $100M revenue, use spreadsheets or other manual finance solutions (Robert Half–linked survey, older). — [IndDist](https://www.inddist.com/home/article/13054962/survey-finds-small-businesses-slow-to-realize-software-benefits)
- Gridfox claims 98% of SMEs use spreadsheets for mission-critical operations. The report is undated and vendor-commissioned. — [Gridfox](https://gridfox.com/whitepaper)
- QuickBooks Desktop: Intuit stopped selling new Pro Plus, Premier Plus and Mac Plus subscriptions in September 2024, while Enterprise is still sold. Desktop 2023 lost support (payroll, bank feeds, security updates) on May 31, 2026. Desktop 2024 is reportedly supported until about September 2027. No new non-Enterprise versions are planned. — [Insightful Accountant](https://blog.insightfulaccountant.com/quickbooks-desktop-accountant-2023-discontinued-after-may-31-2026); [Beancount](https://beancount.io/blog/2026/07/12/quickbooks-desktop-2023-end-of-support-migration-guide); [WizCommerce](https://wizcommerce.com/quickbooks-desktop-discontinued-everything-you-need-to-know/)
- Microsoft: Exchange Server 2016/2019, Skype for Business Server 2015/2019 and Office 2016/2019 reached end of support on October 14, 2025. — [Microsoft Learn](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/lifecycle/announcements/october-14-2025-products-end-of-support)
- Exchange paid ESUs reportedly end in October 2026 with no third period (secondary source). — [WindowsForum](https://windowsforum.com/threads/exchange-2016-2019-esus-end-october-2026-no-period-3.439813/)
- Microsoft Publisher reaches end of support on October 13, 2026, and is being removed from M365. — [The Register](https://www.theregister.com/2026/01/12/microsoft_2026_culls/)
- SQL Server 2016 and SharePoint Server 2016/2019 reached end of support on July 14, 2026. — [TechRepublic](https://www.techrepublic.com/article/news-microsoft-2026-support-deadlines/)
- Weebly's share of websites fell 15% year over year, a declining legacy builder. — [Colorlib](https://colorlib.com/wp/website-builder-market-share/)

### Inferences
- Intake checkboxes should include explicit "manual" options per function: paper or notebook, Excel or Sheets, personal phone or texting, paper checks, fax, on-prem server, desktop software, and "my accountant or bookkeeper handles it". These are likely the most common answers among micro-businesses.
- Flag as sunsetting or discontinued in the form:
  - QuickBooks Desktop Pro/Premier, with 2023 versions already unsupported
  - Peachtree, a brand now Sage 50
  - Office 2016/2019 and Exchange 2016/2019
  - Skype, the consumer service, which Microsoft retired in May 2025 in favor of Teams. This is from my training knowledge and was not verified here.
  - Publisher
  - Bench Accounting, which collapsed at the end of 2024, also from training knowledge
- Desktop QuickBooks users with on-prem servers and hosted QuickBooks (such as Ace Cloud Hosting) are a distinct segment that needs a special automation path.

### Gaps
- I found no 2024–2026 survey quantifying fax use, paper-form use or on-prem server prevalence among U.S. SMBs.
- I found no current payroll-specific paper-check percentage. The Fed data covers B2B payments.
- I could not confirm the exact Desktop 2024 end-of-support date against Intuit's official page; sources say "September 30, 2027" or just "2027".

## Q3. Which tools have open APIs/integration support vs. closed ecosystems?

### Takeaway
Modern cloud SaaS almost universally has public APIs and Zapier apps, and Zapier's catalog of 7,000–8,000+ apps is roughly 4x Make's and far larger than n8n's native set. The automation-hostile segment is legacy desktop and on-prem software (QuickBooks Desktop, Sage 50, Tally, on-prem PBX, fax, paper), plus partner-gated platforms such as Toast and some payroll providers that require approval.

### Cited Findings
- Zapier offers 7,000–8,000+ integrations, Make about 1,500–2,000+, and n8n about 400+ native nodes, or about 1,200 with community nodes. n8n can be self-hosted, and generic HTTP and webhook nodes cover any REST API. — [DigitalApplied](https://www.digitalapplied.com/blog/zapier-vs-make-vs-n8n-2026); [Achiya](https://achiya-automation.com/en/blog/n8n-vs-make-vs-zapier/); [Digidop](https://www.digidop.com/blog/n8n-vs-make-vs-zapier)
- Activepieces lists 680+ integrations plus 400+ MCP servers, a sign that MCP is emerging as the AI-agent integration layer. — [RapidDevelopers](https://www.rapidevelopers.com/md/vs/zapier-alternatives)
- Toast's API license is "limited, revocable, non-transferable", restricted to a "Permitted API Use" tied to Toast's approval of the application, with rate and scope limits. — [Toast API Terms](https://pos.toasttab.com/api-terms-of-use)

### Inferences
Integration-feasibility tiers for automation planning (researcher classification, verify per tool):

## Q4. What are common migration paths?

### Takeaway
The dominant 2025–2026 forced migrations are QuickBooks Desktop to QuickBooks Online (or Xero, or a hosted or Enterprise Desktop), on-prem Exchange and Office 2016/2019 to Microsoft 365 or Google Workspace, and paper or Excel to cloud SaaS. Price-driven migrations include Mailchimp to Klaviyo in e-commerce, ADP or Paychex to Gusto or Rippling, and legacy terminals to Square, Clover or Toast.

### Inferences
Typical migration ladders to use as "current to next step" mappings in the intake:
- **Accounting:** paper or Excel → QuickBooks Desktop or Sage 50 → QBO or Xero → QBO with AI agents (Intuit Assist) or AI bookkeeping services.
- **POS:** cash register or bank terminal → Square or Clover (Toast for restaurants).

- **Website:** GoDaddy, Weebly or static HTML → Wix, Squarespace or WordPress → Shopify for commerce.
- **Automation:** none → Zapier → Make or n8n for cost or self-hosting → AI agents with MCP.
